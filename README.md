## Why Melody Craft exists

Melody Craft is an offline desktop audio-to-MIDI workbench for songwriters, producers, and instrumentalists turning separately recorded vocal or solo instrument takes into expressive MIDI on Windows x64 and Linux x64.

> **Edition boundary:** The technical sections below describe the complete product surface and can include optional licensed workflows. The exact free Community Edition scope is listed separately below.

### Capture the performance, not a stripped-down skeleton

Three ways Melody Craft preserves musical expression from the first drop to final DAW import.

### Preserve slides and vibrato

Capture expressive bends, scoops, and vibrato as stepped MIDI pitch-bend curves overlaid directly on each piano-roll note, with per-note cent span readouts and overlap-safe bend centering.

### Audition with level-matched A/B

Switch immediately between Recording and MIDI playback at matched perceived loudness using built-in preview voices while tracking the aligned waveform and piano-roll playhead.

### Refine on a dedicated piano roll

Edit notes in Select or Draw mode with a configurable snap grid, tempo setting, velocity stem lane, split and merge tools, multi-note phrase nudging, and a live Take Summary inspector.

### Tune recognition and work offline

Choose Auto, Cleaner, or Detailed cleaning modes with adjustable onset and sustain thresholds plus a Keep bends toggle, while keeping every recording and .mcraft project strictly on your own machine.

### Keep vocal scoops and bass slides intact

- Stepped pitch-bend curves stay visible right on every note bar so you can verify slides and vibrato instead of guessing what your synth will receive.
- Overlap-safe bend centering prevents a bent transition from pulling an overlapping chord note out of tune on a single MIDI channel.

### Catch mistakes by ear in seconds

- Toggle between Recording and MIDI at matched loudness without touching a fader, keeping volume differences from masking timing or pitch errors.
- Switch preview voices such as Grand Piano or Electric Bass to check attack clarity and low-register phrasing against the aligned waveform.

### Stay strictly local from draft to export

- All decoding, note detection, bend extraction, piano-roll editing, and .mcraft project storage run locally with zero cloud uploads or accounts.
- Try full transcription, A/B comparison, and note editing in Community Edition, then unlock Standard MIDI and WAV export with a Website Direct license (Professional or Team) or Microsoft Store unlock.

## Frequently asked questions

> **Community scope:** Community Edition keeps offline transcription, level-matched Recording vs MIDI A/B playback, piano-roll editing, and project work free; exporting MIDI and WAV audio requires a licensed edition.

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Melody Craft upload my recordings to the cloud?</strong></summary>

No. Audio decoding, note recognition, pitch-bend extraction, preview playback, piano-roll editing, and .mcraft project storage all run 100% locally and offline on your Windows or Linux computer with no account required.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can Melody Craft split a full finished song into separate instruments?</strong></summary>

No. Melody Craft is built specifically for separately recorded pitched sources such as a solo vocal, electric bass, guitar melody, bowed or wind instrument, or synth lead. Full mixes and unpitched drums are outside its scope.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does Melody Craft handle vocal slides, vibrato, and bass bends?</strong></summary>

When Keep slides and vibrato is enabled, Melody Craft writes expressive pitch motion as stepped MIDI pitch-bend curves overlaid right on each note. The inspector shows cent span values, and overlapping notes automatically center the channel bend.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which audio file formats can I convert to MIDI?</strong></summary>

You can drop or import WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, and AAC files. Clean, dry mono recordings of a single pitched instrument or voice give the clearest note boundaries and bend contours, while heavy reverb or distortion may require light manual editing.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which Melody Craft installer should I download for Windows or Linux?</strong></summary>

On Windows 10 or later (x64), download the official Windows x64 installer (.exe) from the download page or install Melody Craft from Microsoft Store. On Linux x64, pick DEB, RPM, or portable AppImage. Every website download includes a verified SHA-256 checksum.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do the Auto, Cleaner, and Detailed recognition modes differ?</strong></summary>

Auto balances note clarity and detail for most clean solo takes, Cleaner suppresses short room-bleed or breath artifacts, and Detailed preserves rapid grace notes and fine articulations. You can pair any mode with Onset and Sustain threshold adjustments and reconvert locally in seconds.

</details>
